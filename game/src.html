He says what you doPull, pick, unlock, take or examine anything, and Percival draws a breath, then says it out loud.

His ringHis voice carries to the edge of a ring around Mouse, always the same size; walls cut it short. Stand at anything you can use and the ring shows first. Red means a guard is inside it.

Guards who heargo to look at whatever he talked about. So get him talking about something over there, and slip past.

SightsStep into a gold ring and he reads out everything there. His ring follows Mouse while he reads.

Your feet and their eyesWalking close to a guard, or running nearby, makes him turn. Sneaking is silent: Shift turns it on and off. Guards see in a cone, and best in torchlight. The crawlspace is too low for them.
